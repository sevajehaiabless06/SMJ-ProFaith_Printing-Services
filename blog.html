Printing Tips

Learn simple ways to prepare your files, choose materials, and understand basic printing requirements.

🖨️

Check Your File Before Printing

Review your document carefully before sending it for printing. Check spelling, text, images, page size, and layout to avoid unnecessary corrections or reprints.

📄

Choose the Right Size

Identify the required paper or material size before placing your order. Providing the correct dimensions helps ensure that your finished print matches your intended design.

🔍

Use Clear and High-Quality Images

When preparing designs with photos, logos, or graphics, use clear and high-resolution images whenever possible to achieve better printing results.

🎨

Review Your Design Colors

Check the colors and overall appearance of your design before submitting it. This helps ensure that your printing requirements are properly communicated.
