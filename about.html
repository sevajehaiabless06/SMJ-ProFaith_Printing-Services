About Us

Learn more about our company, our purpose, and our commitment to providing quality printing services.

Behind the Business

SMJ Profaith Printing Services is a local printing business in Tunasan, Muntinlupa, established on February 22, 2020, where ideas come to life in vibrant color and creativity. Whether customers are preparing for an event or simply need a standout print, the shop offers printing products and services for personal, school, business, and event needs.

The business is guided by its motto, "Printing with Purpose. Earning Profit by Faith." For SMJ Profaith, this means delivering exceptional quality, accuracy, and dependable service with a touch of faith and a lot of creativity. Customers can walk in or reach out through Facebook, Messenger, Instagram, or email.

Owned by Sarah Mae Daan Reyes, Mari-Joy Gargallano, and Joemarie Gargallano, SMJ Profaith Printing Services continues to provide fast, reliable, and affordable printing services to the community, and invites customers to ask how it can help bring their ideas to life.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide the community with fast, reliable, and affordable printing services while maintaining quality, accuracy, and excellent customer service in every transaction.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and leading printing shop known for efficient service, modernized business operations, and customer satisfaction within the community it serves.

Our Objectives

SMJ Profaith Printing Services aims to achieve the following objectives:

  1. Fast, Reliable, and Affordable Services

    Provide fast, reliable, and affordable printing services that meet customer needs and expectations.

  2. Quality and Accuracy

    Maintain high standards of quality and accuracy in every printing job and transaction.

  3. Long-Term Customer Relationships

    Build long-term relationships with customers through excellent and consistent service.

  4. Trusted Business Reputation

    Strengthen the shop's reputation as a trusted and dependable printing service provider in the community.
